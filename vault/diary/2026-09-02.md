## 写作

雅思大作文第一段模板表达：

-   第一句：背景
    
-   第二句：改写题目
    
-   第三句：立场/结构
    

大作文一般只有五类题目：

-   同意/不同意：Some people think university education should be free for everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
    
    -   In many countries / In recent years, \[话题\] has become a serious concern / a widely discussed issue.
        
        It is often argued that \[题目核心改写\].
        
        I largely agree / disagree with this view because \[一句话理由方向\].
        
    -   In many countries, the cost of higher education has become a serious concern. It is often argued that university tuition should be free for all students. I largely agree with this view because equal access to education benefits both individuals and society.
        

-   讨论双方：Some people believe that children should start learning a foreign language at primary school. Others think it is better to wait until secondary school. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
    
    -   \[话题\] is a widely debated topic / has attracted much public attention.
        
        Some people argue that \[观点A改写\], while others believe that \[观点B改写\].
        
        This essay will discuss both views before giving my own opinion.
        
    -   The best age to begin foreign language learning is a widely debated topic. Some people argue that children should start in primary school, while others believe secondary school is more suitable. This essay will discuss both views before giving my own opinion.
        

-   利弊：In some countries, many people work from home. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?
    
    -   \[话题\] has become increasingly common in recent years.
        
        Although this trend / arrangement has some drawbacks, I believe its advantages outweigh the disadvantages.
        
        This essay will examine both sides.
        
    -   Working from home has become increasingly common in recent years. Although this arrangement has some drawbacks, I believe its advantages outweigh the disadvantages. This essay will examine both sides.
        

-   问题 / 原因 / 解决：In many cities, traffic congestion is a serious problem. What are the causes of this, and what solutions can you suggest?
    
-   双问题：More and more people are choosing to live alone. Why is this happening? Is it a positive or negative development?
